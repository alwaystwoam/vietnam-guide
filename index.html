A travel itinerary

Vietnam

Two weeks to fall in love.

Saigon — Da Nang & Hoi An — the southern coast — Saigon. For DK & Maria. Late February to April.

Editor's note

Vietnam is best experienced in layers. The velvet heat of a Saigon evening; scooters flowing past colonial facades and glass towers; iced coffee sweating on a cafe table; tailors and tiny boutiques tucked down leafy streets; rooftops where the whole city seems to flicker on at once.

Then the trip opens out. The long pale beaches of Da Nang. Lanterns floating through warm Hoi An nights. Spa mornings wrapped in bamboo and sea air. The blue-water resort rhythm of Nha Trang and the southern coast.

Travelers describe the country less like a checklist and more like a feeling: the first street crossing that seems impossible until it suddenly makes sense, the kindness of a cafe owner, a bowl of noodles that costs almost nothing but stays in memory. The route below keeps DK and Maria in the comfortable lane — beautiful hotels, good coffee, brunch, private transfers, soft culture, sunset drinks, beach time, and just enough market color and local texture to make the country feel alive. The rhythm: city, coast, romance, reset, and a little adventure to remember it properly.

Chapter One

I.

Saigon

Days 01 — 04 · The big-city chapter

Saigon is the trip's accelerant — a hot, humming opening volume of cafes and rooftops, scooters and tailors, polished hotel lobbies and pavement noodle stands within the same block. The plan here is to ease in, not battle the city: arrival night gentle, mornings late, afternoons spent in air-conditioned cafes and Thao Dien boutiques, evenings on a rooftop watching the lights come on. Four nights at The Reverie Saigon in District 1 keeps everything walkable.

Treat the days as a rhythm, not a checklist. Anchor each morning with brunch and coffee; let the afternoon roam shopping or culture; finish with a long dinner and one rooftop drink.

Chapter Two

II.

The Central Coast

Days 05 — 09 · Da Nang & Hoi An

This is the emotional center of the trip. Five nights at Naman Retreat on Non Nuoc Beach, a daily couples treatment at the Pure Spa, beach mornings, and one luminous evening across the river in Hoi An. The pace drops; the days shape themselves around breakfast, a soft activity, spa, and a slow dinner.

Hoi An is the centerpiece. Don't try to do it in the heat — go after four in the afternoon, walk the yellow-walled lanes, drink herbal tea on Tran Phu, eat slowly, and cross into the night market when the lanterns come on.

Chapter Three

The Beach Chapter

Appendix & Reference

Hotels, cafes, and notes for the road.

Current-map check

Small places change quickly

Cafes, boutiques, spas, and night-market stalls can move or rebrand quickly in Vietnam. The guide uses stronger anchors, but DK and Maria should still confirm opening hours and recent Google Maps reviews the week they travel.

Cafe Apartments rule

Treat 42 Nguyen Hue as the experience: balcony views, stairwell wandering, and a drink over the walking street. Pick the best currently open cafe on the day rather than chasing an old named listing.

Boutique rule

For Thao Dien, start with In The Mood, Saigon Concept, KOKONIC, Amaï, Bakes, and Maison Marou, then let recent map reviews decide the exact order.

How to get the Bali / Maldives feeling
  • Room category matters more than the name. Choose a private-pool villa, oceanfront villa, or club ocean-view room before chasing every brand.
  • Don't one-night the dream resort. Six Senses or Amanoi only make sense for three nights; otherwise the transfers eat the magic.
  • Keep one city-beach chapter. InterContinental Nha Trang stays the best option for Tran Phu walks, Café Culture brunch, and Sailing Club sunset.
  • Best balanced upgrade. Keep Naman in Da Nang, then swap InterContinental for The Anam, Fusion, or Six Senses depending on budget.
  • Most romantic splurge path. Naman or TIA in Da Nang, then Six Senses Ninh Van Bay for the private-pool bay escape.

Travel tips

Entry, apps, food & logistics

Every trip needs a thin layer of operational knowledge. Read once; refer back as needed.

Before you go
Polish passport
Tourism guidance lists Poland for 45-day visa-free travel through 14 August 2028. Re-check before booking.
Passport validity
Six-plus months beyond the trip; at least one blank page.
E-visa backup
If rules change or dates extend, use Vietnam's official e-visa site only.
Travel insurance
Medical, domestic flight delays, lost luggage, and activity coverage.
Best season
Default target is late February through April.
Apps & connectivity
  • Grab — for city rides and food delivery. Set it up before landing.
  • eSIM — install before flying, activate on arrival.
  • Google Maps — pin hotels, airports, restaurants, spas, rainy-day options.
  • Google Translate — useful for menus, drivers, photo translation.
Getting around
  • Domestic flights — Saigon → Da Nang, Da Nang → Cam Ranh, Cam Ranh → Saigon are the comfort defaults.
  • VIP airport fast track — book arrival meet-and-greet for SGN; consider it again for international departure.
  • Private cars — for airport transfers, Hoi An evenings, beach outings, and the final Saigon hotel transfer.
  • No scooters — Grab, private cars, and guided experiences are better.
  • Airport timing — about two hours early for domestic; extra buffer before the flight home.
Food strategy for picky eaters
  • Use hotel breakfast as the daily anchor.
  • Prioritize cafes, Italian, steak, brunch, rooftops, hotel restaurants, and polished Vietnamese restaurants.
  • Local safe tries: banh mi, pho bo, pho ga, com tam, grilled pork, chicken rice, mild Mi Quang.
  • Avoid seafood by default. Useful phrase: khong hai san means no seafood.
  • Useful spice phrase: khong cay means not spicy.

Where to splurge

A strong Saigon hotel; VIP airport fast track on arrival; spa at Naman or TIA; private-pool villa nights in Cam Ranh or Ninh Van Bay; rooftops; private cars on transfer days.

Where to save

Hotel breakfast, Grab instead of taxis, cafes instead of tasting menus, no over-packed tours.

Cash shortcut

As of 2 May 2026: $100 ≈ 2.63M VND ≈ 362 PLN. 1M VND ≈ $38 ≈ 137 PLN.

Bonus

Swap-ins & add-ons

Useful alternatives if weather, flights, or curiosity changes the route.

Hanoi add-on

Use only as a 2-night extension, not squeezed into the core route. Anchor at Grand Hotel du Lac; walk the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, and the French Quarter.

Phu Quoc winter beach swap

Swap for Nha Trang in a less ideal central-coast weather window. Regent or New World for resort days; Sunset Town, Sailing Club Phu Quoc, and spa villas.

Cam Ranh luxury upgrade

If they want the Nha Trang chapter to feel more private. Bai Dai, private-pool villas, spa, airport convenience.

Da Lat mountain add-on

Trade one beach night and the final Saigon night for two nights in Da Lat. Colline Da Lat; Xuan Huong Lake, the Datanla alpine coaster, and the night market.

Mekong day trip

A softer countryside option from Saigon. Use on Day 02 only if they want more local texture. Book a reputable private operator; keep the day comfortable, not rugged.
